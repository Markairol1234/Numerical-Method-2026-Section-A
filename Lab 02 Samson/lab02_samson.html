Analytical Derivatives of the Fitted Function

The first derivative of the logistic curve gives the continuous filling rate. Its maximum occurs at the logistic inflection point t₀.

dh/dt = a k exp[-k(t − t₀)] / [1 + exp(-k(t − t₀))]²
"http://www.w3.org/Graphics/SVG/1.1/DTD/svg11.dtd">
d²h/dt² = a k² exp[-k(t − t₀)] [exp(-k(t − t₀)) − 1] / [1 + exp(-k(t − t₀))]³
"http://www.w3.org/Graphics/SVG/1.1/DTD/svg11.dtd">
Interpretation: The second derivative is positive before the inflection point and negative afterward, indicating that the filling rate first increases, reaches its maximum at the inflection time, and then decreases as the reservoir level approaches the settling level.

Selected Model

h(t) = c + a / [1 + exp(-k(t − t₀))]

The four-parameter logistic was selected because the raw reservoir record represents a single filling event with a gradual transition toward a higher level, making a sigmoidal model with one inflection point physically reasonable. The data do not show clear evidence requiring two separate filling pulses, while a cubic polynomial is only a mathematical baseline rather than a physically motivated reservoir model.

Initial Guess

Parameter Initial value Basis from actual data
c 14.180000 m Observed minimum stage
a 0.330000 m Observed maximum minus observed minimum
k 0.242424 1/h 4 × local midpoint slope / observed stage range; local slope = 0.020000 m/h
t₀ 10.250000 h Time of observation closest to midpoint stage (14.3450 m)

The fit uses scipy.optimize.curve_fit with method="lm" and maxfev=20000. No parameter bounds are used because Levenberg–Marquardt does not accept bounds.

Residual Analysis

The residuals are centered around zero overall (mean = 0.000000 m), but there are runs of 7 consecutive positive residuals from 6.50 to 8.00 h and 7 consecutive negative residuals from 10.50 to 12.00 h. The residual spread does not increase as fitted level rises; the standard deviations for low, middle, and high fitted levels are 0.0148, 0.0140, and 0.0118 m, respectively. The largest absolute residual is 0.0352 m, which exceeds the logger's 0.01 m resolution by 0.0252 m.
